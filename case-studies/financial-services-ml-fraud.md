# Case Study: Financial-Services ML Fraud Detection

## Context
A cross-functional team of data scientists, data engineers, fraud analysts, and delivery stakeholders supported a real-time card-transaction fraud detection capability in a regulated payments environment.

## Delivery and governance focus
The work included:
- identifying and validating data sources,
- protecting sensitive cardholder information,
- preparing data for supervised learning,
- supporting an ML model used for fraud risk scoring,
- implementing human oversight,
- monitoring false positives and performance,
- maintaining traceability and governance evidence.

## Human oversight
Fraud analysts reviewed alerts and blocked transactions. Where false positives were identified, thresholds or temporary suppression rules could be adjusted under controlled processes.

Customer verification signals were also used to improve the feedback loop.

## Monitoring
Post-deployment oversight included:
- scoring accuracy,
- false-positive rate,
- decision traceability,
- documentation of data sources and model behavior,
- review of governance controls.

## Product lesson
Production AI needs more than predictive performance. It also needs:
- clear ownership,
- human review,
- customer-impact controls,
- operational monitoring,
- and traceable decisions.

> Portfolio note: This case study is generalized and contains no confidential client or employer data.
