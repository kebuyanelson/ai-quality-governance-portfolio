# Case Study: Enterprise AI/ML Platform Governance

## Context
An enterprise R&D platform enabled data scientists and engineers to run AI/ML workloads on secure Kubernetes-based GPU infrastructure.

## Governance focus
The work centered on embedding governance into the development lifecycle rather than reviewing systems only after they were built.

Key elements included:
- standardized AI intake,
- risk assessment,
- AI inventory,
- business ownership,
- risk classification,
- recertification,
- privacy-by-design,
- access controls,
- explainability,
- performance/drift monitoring.

## GenAI considerations
Emerging GenAI initiatives required additional controls such as:
- prompt governance,
- prompt-injection testing,
- PII filtering,
- vendor security/privacy review,
- bias and safety considerations.

## Delivery model
Governance requirements were translated into actionable delivery activities and embedded into Agile workflows across distributed technical teams.

## Product lesson
AI governance is most effective when it becomes part of the product lifecycle and delivery process rather than a final approval step.

> Portfolio note: This case study is generalized and contains no confidential client or employer data.
