# AI Governance KPI Framework

The goal of governance measurement is not only to count activity, but to understand whether governance is **effective, efficient, complete, and trusted**.

## Business KPIs

### Percentage of AI use cases formally reviewed and approved
Shows whether AI activity is entering the formal process rather than becoming shadow AI.

### Time to governance decision
Measures whether governance enables delivery efficiently or creates avoidable delay.

### Percentage of high-risk use cases with required controls
Measures whether risk-based controls are actually implemented.

### Audit / regulatory issue rate
Tracks repeated control gaps and whether the framework is improving over time.

## Technical KPIs

### Performance against approved thresholds
Shows whether the system remains inside the performance range approved for use.

### Drift rate
Tracks data, concept, and model drift after deployment.

### Fairness stability
Monitors subgroup performance where fairness is relevant.

### Groundedness / hallucination rate
For GenAI, measures whether outputs are supported by approved evidence.

## Operational KPIs

### AI inventory completeness
Measures visibility of the enterprise AI estate.

### Documentation completeness
Tracks the presence of intake records, risk assessments, approvals, validation evidence, and monitoring plans.

### Monitoring coverage
Measures the proportion of systems actively monitored for relevant quality and risk signals.

### Recertification completion rate
Tracks whether periodic reviews and material-change reassessments occur on schedule.

## Maturity signal

### Stakeholder governance sentiment
Structured feedback from business, technology, risk, privacy, legal, and other stakeholders can reveal whether governance is clear, usable, trusted, and supportive of delivery.
